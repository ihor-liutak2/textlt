Independent by Design

Developed in Ukraine. Designed for an international community.

TextLT is an independent software project for creating, editing, organizing, processing, and exchanging textual information. It combines plain-text editing with document workflows, personal notes, and reusable operations so people can work with information on their own terms.

An Independent Software Project

TextLT began as a terminal editor with familiar shortcuts, menus, and direct text selection. The earliest recorded source commit is from June 2026. It has grown into a native C++ terminal workspace built around Documents, Notes, and Actions. A project sets the folder context and can connect it to dedicated Notes and Actions groups and a saved remote connection.

The project combines established tools with application-level engineering shaped by practical use. Its direction is to improve everyday work with text while keeping the editor approachable.

Our Mission

Make working with text convenient, efficient, accessible, and independent. Bring documents, notes, and reusable operations together so people can choose how to organize their work.

Our Vision

Keep textual information portable, accessible over time, and under the user’s control. Extend what people can do across projects and devices while preserving the simplicity and flexibility of terminal-native editing.

Ukrainian Origins. International Perspective.

TextLT is an independent Ukrainian software project built through practical engineering and experimentation. It is designed for people who work in different languages, disciplines, and operating environments. Portability and configurable interaction help users bring the same approach to text work across those settings.

Independent Software for an Interconnected Europe

TextLT’s development goals include user-controlled storage, open formats, documented interfaces, multilingual interaction, accessibility, and maintainable software. These values offer common ground for independent developers and cross-border research and engineering collaboration in Europe and beyond.

Built Through Software Engineering

TextLT uses C++17 and FTXUI for terminal interaction. Document sessions and pane state have separate responsibilities. Project context, configurable shortcuts and themes, and built-in and Lua processors support different workflows. CMake, CTest, and technical references support ongoing development.

Development Beyond Individual Features
